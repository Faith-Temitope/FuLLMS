This guide explains how to use the FUL Integrated Learning Management System (ILMS) as a student.

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to the site address given by your department (e.g. `http://<site-address>/moodle`).
2. Enter the username/email and password given to you, or the one you set when you registered.
3. If you forget your password, use **"Forgotten your username or password?"** on the login page.

## 2. Your Dashboard

After logging in you land on your **Dashboard**, which lists:
- The courses you are enrolled on.
- Upcoming assignment deadlines.
- Recent announcements from your lecturers.

Click any course to open it.

## 3. Finding course material

Inside a course, materials are listed in the order your lecturer added them, grouped by week or topic. Two kinds of material appear:

- **Ordinary files** (PDF, slides, etc.) added directly by your lecturer.
- **Accessible Material** activities (look for the accessibility icon) — these have been automatically checked for accessibility (captions on videos, alternative text on images/documents) before you could see them. If a lecturer uploads something that fails this check, you will not see it at all until they fix it — you are only ever shown material that has passed.

Click a material's name to open it. Video material shows a player with captions (if provided); images show with a text description underneath; documents and other files download to your device.

## 4. Submitting assignments

1. Open the course and click the assignment.
2. Read the instructions and deadline shown on the page.
3. Click **Add submission**, upload your file (or type your answer, depending on how your lecturer set it up), and click **Save changes**.
4. You can update your submission any time before the deadline unless your lecturer has disabled that.
5. Once your lecturer grades it, you'll see your grade and any feedback on the assignment page and on your **Grades** page (accessible from the course menu).

## 5. Announcements

Each course has an **Announcements** forum. Your lecturer posts important updates there, and you get a notification automatically — check your notification bell (top right) or your email.

## 6. Messaging your lecturer

Click the **Messages** icon (speech bubble, top right) to send a direct message to your lecturer or classmates without leaving the platform.

## 7. Accessibility settings

Click **"Accessibility settings"** (fixed at the top-right of every page) to set, once, for your account:

- **Screen reader optimised mode** — stronger keyboard focus indicators, always-visible skip links, no animations.
- **Text size** — Default, Large, or Extra large, applied across the whole site.
- **High contrast mode** — black background, white text, bright yellow links, for maximum readability.
- **Show video captions by default** — whether captions switch on automatically when you open a video.

These settings are saved to your account and apply on every device you log in from.

## 8. Using it on your phone

- The site works directly in your phone's browser — no app required.
- You can also install the official **Moodle** app (Android/iOS) and enter the site address to log in from a native app with offline access to some content.

## Getting help

If something isn't working, contact your course lecturer or the department's ICT/admin contact.
