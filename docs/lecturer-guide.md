This guide explains how to use the FUL Integrated Learning Management System (ILMS) as a lecturer.

## 1. Logging in and your courses

Log in at the site address given by your department. Your Dashboard lists the courses you teach. If a course you should be teaching isn't listed, contact your department's LMS administrator to be enrolled as a Teacher on it.

## 2. Turning editing on

Open your course and click **"Turn editing on"** (top right, or in the course menu). This reveals **Add an activity or resource** links in every section, and edit/move/delete controls on everything already there. Click it again to turn editing off once you're done — students never see editing controls.

## 3. Uploading accessible course material

This is the key difference from a plain file upload, and it's how the platform enforces accessibility automatically:

1. With editing on, click **Add an activity or resource** in the section you want, then choose **Accessible Material**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — what students will see as the activity name.
   - **Description** (optional) — shown above the material.
   - **File** — PDF, DOCX, MP4, MOV, JPG, PNG or TXT, up to 200MB.
   - **Caption file** — required if your file is a video. Upload a `.vtt` or `.srt` subtitle file alongside it.
   - **Alternative text** — required if your file is an image or document. Describe what it shows/contains in at least 5 characters (screen readers read this aloud).
3. Click **Save and return to course**.

**What happens next:**
- If a video has no caption file, or an image/document has no (or too-short) alternative text, the system **flags** it: students will not see it at all, and you'll see a warning banner explaining exactly what's missing when you open it.
- Fix the issue by editing the activity (add the caption file or alt text) and saving again — it's automatically re-checked.
- Once it passes, it's published immediately and every student enrolled on the course gets a notification.

Plain PDFs/DOCX/images you upload as an ordinary **File** resource (instead of Accessible Material) are not checked — use Accessible Material whenever the content should be accessibility-verified.

## 4. Assignments and grading

1. **Add an activity or resource → Assignment.**
2. Set the title, instructions, and **due date**. Students cannot submit after the due date unless you change it or grant an extension.
3. To grade: open the assignment and click **View all submissions**. Click a student's submission to open the grading screen, enter a grade and feedback, and save. Grades are only visible to the student once you release them.

## 5. Announcements

Every course has an **Announcements** forum (visible at the top of the course by default). Post there for anything all enrolled students need to see — it also sends them a notification/email automatically. This is the main replacement for course WhatsApp groups.

## 6. Messaging students

Use the **Messages** icon (top right) to message a student directly, or use the participants list inside your course to message several students at once.

## 7. Checking accessibility compliance

The Accessibility Compliance status of each Accessible Material activity is visible on the course's activity index page (**Accessible Materials** in the course's activity list), showing Passed / Flagged for each item at a glance.

## 8. Getting help

Contact the LMS administrator for account, enrolment, or technical issues.
